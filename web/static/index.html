Add a custom AI provider

Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works — a local vLLM/LM Studio server, NVIDIA NIM, OpenRouter, or your own gateway. Ollama and OpenCode have dedicated modes.

How to use DocExtract

  1. Upload one or many files at once — click the drop area (you can multi-select), drag them onto the window, or paste from your clipboard. Each file is processed asynchronously with its own progress.
  2. Choose the language (Auto covers English, বাংলা and हिन्दी) and the engine. Auto uses the accurate PaddleOCR-VL model when available.
  3. Watch the live progress, browse page previews, then copy, download or share the extracted text.

Tip: use the ← → arrow keys to flip through pages and ⌘/Ctrl+K to upload quickly.
